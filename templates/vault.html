What Tripwire can and can't see

Can't see
A locked item's file, title, note, or words. Your vault passphrase. Your recovery code. The key that locks everything.
Can see
That a locked item exists, roughly how big it is, and when it was added. That's needed to keep it and to count your space.
How it's locked
AES-256-GCM, built into your browser (no outside service). Your passphrase becomes a key through 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2-SHA256, so guessing it is slow. The locking code is open: it's this one file, and the same file is in your “Take my shelf with me” download.
Taking it with you
The ZIP download includes your locked items, still locked, and a page that unlocks them with your passphrase, offline, with no internet and no Tripwire.
The honest limit
Like every website that locks things in your browser, you're trusting that the page sent to you is the real one. If Tripwire's server were ever taken over, a changed page could try to catch your passphrase as you type it. Items already locked stay locked. For the most sensitive things, keep them off the internet entirely.
