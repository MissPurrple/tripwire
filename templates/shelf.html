My shelf

Your own copies of the documents and pages you care about: a gazette, a memo, a Wikipedia article, a blog post. If the original is changed or taken down, your copy stays. And you can take the whole shelf with you as one file, to keep on a phone or a USB stick, with no internet and no Tripwire needed.

Take my shelf with me

Download everything on your shelf: the original files, their words, and a page for each, all in one file.

ZIP: unzip it and open index.html in any web browser. ZIM: open it with Kiwix, the free app for reading whole libraries offline, on phones and computers. It's searchable inside Kiwix, and a Kiwix hotspot can share it over Wi-Fi with everyone nearby, like a school or a community centre.
