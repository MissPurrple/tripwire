## Description to post

**Tripwire: catch the internet rewriting history**

Information about Indigenous communities, minority languages and local histories is thin online, and what exists can be quietly edited, poisoned or deleted. As AI learns from the web, whatever survives becomes "the truth."

Tripwire watches the Wikipedia articles and web pages you care about. When something changes, it doesn't spam you. It saves the before and after into your personal archive, asks the Internet Archive to keep a copy, judges whether it was a typo fix or a large removal, and flags names whose spelling changed (a quiet sign of a language spreading or fading). Once a week you get a summary. Keep what matters; disregard the rest.

A working first version is already built (Python + Flask). Next: automatic scheduled checks, offline preservation with Kiwix, and a weekly summary bot.
