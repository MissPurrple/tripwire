# Problem case for the pitch: Artizen (October 2026)

Purple's ask (30 Oct 2026): "use Artizen as a problem case/evidence of a site that went down without notice, with the
'playbook' wording being changed before artists and those raising had to find out their money was gone."

## What's well supported
- Artizen (artizen.fund, Artizen Works Inc.) was a match-funding platform for creative projects: $10 Artifacts unlocked
  match funding and prizes. Rules lived in the Artizen Playbook (play.artizen.fund).
- 2026-10-05: the founder emailed creators that Artizen would stop operations: "We're paying out Artifact sales, but not
  match funding or cash prizes." (as pasted in community groups; The ZAO's record)
- By 2026-10-09 play.artizen.fund redirected to an "Artizen Payouts" site; the live Playbook was gone. Claims and wallet
  withdrawals deadline: 2026-11-30, 11:59 PM PT.
- The Playbook copy of 2026-10-02 adds: Artizen itself may elect Early Exit for any or all projects; a Platform Continuity
  section; a "full satisfaction and release" clause. The Playbook's own version history (to 2026-09-04) describes Early
  Exit ("Rage Quit" renamed) only as the creator's choice.

## Not established (don't claim it)
- WHEN the Artizen-elected Early Exit wording was added. Earliest copy with it: 2026-10-02. Community says mid/late
  September: UNVERIFIED.
- The reason for stopping (personal to the founder; leave it out).
- Platform totals ($8.3M raised in Season 6) are Artizen's own claim, unverified.

## Honest limit for Tripwire
The Playbook reportedly loaded its text from a database after the page opened, so Wayback captures are often empty.
Tripwire's plain page reading has the same problem. Say so. Workaround today: save the page from the browser as a PDF and
keep it on My shelf (stamped as uploaded). Future: read pages the way a browser does.
